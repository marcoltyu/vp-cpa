Our work

工作範圍

We help management identify the accounting, tax, documentation and corporate implications of a proposed transaction or operational change before it is finalised.

我們協助管理層在交易或營運變更落實前，識別相關會計、稅務、文件及公司事務影響。

Frequently asked questions

常見問題

When should a CPA be involved in a business decision?企業決策應在甚麼時候諮詢會計師？

Professional input is most useful before the transaction or arrangement is finalised, when accounting, tax, documentation and structuring options can still be considered.

在交易或安排尚未落實前尋求專業意見最有價值，因為當時仍可考慮會計、稅務、文件及架構選項。

Can you review a proposed restructuring?可以審閱擬議重組嗎？

Yes. We can review the accounting, tax and corporate implications and identify matters requiring legal, valuation or regulatory advice.

可以。我們可審閱會計、稅務及公司層面的影響，並識別需要法律、估值或監管意見的事項。

Do you provide transaction support?有否提供交易支援？

We can assist with financial information review, accounting treatment, tax considerations, documentation requirements and coordination with other advisers.

我們可協助審閱財務資料、會計處理、稅務考量、文件要求，以及與其他顧問協調。

Can you assist with cross border business matters?可以協助跨境業務事項嗎？

We can advise on the Hong Kong accounting, tax and corporate aspects and coordinate with overseas advisers where local advice is required.

我們可就香港的會計、稅務及公司事務提供意見，並在需要當地意見時與海外顧問協調。

Our approach

服務方針

Advice is grounded in the commercial objective and available information. Where legal, valuation or overseas input is needed, the relevant coordination points are identified early.

意見會以商業目標及現有資料為基礎。如需要法律、估值或海外專業意見，會及早識別相關協調事項。
