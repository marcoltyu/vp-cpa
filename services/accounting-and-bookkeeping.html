Our work

工作範圍

We maintain accounting records, prepare bank reconciliations and management accounts, and support year end closing for businesses without a full internal finance function.

我們為未設完整內部財務職能的企業維持會計記錄、編製銀行對賬及管理賬目，並支援年結工作。

How the work progresses

工作流程

01

Capture

記錄

Record transactions using an agreed chart of accounts and document flow.

按議定會計科目及文件流程記錄交易。

02

Reconcile

對賬

Reconcile banks, receivables, payables and key balance sheet items.

對銀行、應收、應付及主要資產負債表項目進行對賬。

03

Report

報告

Prepare management accounts and clear balance sheet breakdowns.

編製管理賬目及清晰資產負債表明細。

04

Close

年結

Post closing entries and prepare records for audit and tax filing.

入賬年結調整並準備審計及稅務申報資料。

Frequently asked questions

常見問題

How frequently should management accounts be prepared?管理賬目應多久編製一次？

The appropriate frequency depends on the business. Monthly accounts are useful for active businesses, while quarterly or year end reporting may be sufficient for simpler entities.

合適頻率視乎業務需要。營運活躍的企業通常適合每月編製，較簡單的實體則可能每季或年結編製已足夠。

Can you help with incomplete historical records?可以協助整理不完整的過往賬目嗎？

Yes. We can assess the available records, identify gaps and agree a catch up plan. The scope and timetable depend on the volume and quality of the information available.

可以。我們可先評估現有記錄、識別缺漏，再議定補賬方案。工作範圍及時間取決於資料數量及完整程度。

Which accounting software should we use?應使用哪一套會計軟件？

The choice should reflect transaction volume, reporting needs, internal controls and integration requirements. We can discuss the practical requirements before a system is selected.

選擇應考慮交易量、報告需要、內部監控及系統整合。我們可在選擇系統前協助釐清實際需要。

Will the records be ready for audit and tax filing?賬目會否整理至可供審計及報稅使用？

Our year end process focuses on reconciled balances, supporting schedules and clear classifications so that the records can be used efficiently for audit and tax work.

我們的年結程序著重已對賬結餘、支援附表及清晰分類，讓會計記錄可有效用於審計及稅務工作。

Our approach

服務方針

The focus is on reconciled balances, consistent classifications and schedules that can be traced back to supporting records. This reduces avoidable reconstruction during audit and tax work.

工作重點是已對賬結餘、一致分類及可追溯至支援記錄的附表，從而減少審計及稅務工作期間不必要的重整。
