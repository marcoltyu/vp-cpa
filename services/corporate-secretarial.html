Our work

Frequently asked questions

常見問題

When is the annual return due?周年申報表何時到期？

The filing deadline depends on the company's type and anniversary date. The annual return should be prepared in advance so that changes can be checked before filing.

申報期限視公司類型及成立周年日而定。應預先準備周年申報表，以便在提交前核對公司變更。

How are changes in directors or shareholders documented?董事或股東變更如何記錄？

The required documents may include board or shareholder approvals, prescribed forms, updated registers and supporting identity or transaction documents.

所需文件可能包括董事會或股東批准、指定表格、更新登記冊，以及身份或交易支援文件。

Can you assist with a share transfer or allotment?可以協助股份轉讓或配發嗎？

Yes. We can prepare the corporate documentation and coordinate the filings. Tax, valuation or legal input may also be required depending on the transaction.

可以。我們可準備公司文件及協調申報。視乎交易情況，亦可能需要稅務、估值或法律意見。

Why do statutory records need to be kept consistent?為何法定記錄必須保持一致？

Consistent records are important for audit, banking, due diligence, ownership changes and future transactions. Differences should be identified and corrected early.

一致的記錄對審計、銀行、盡職審查、擁有權變更及未來交易非常重要。任何差異都應及早識別及修正。

Our approach

服務方針

Corporate records are checked against the intended transaction and required approvals before filing. Material changes are coordinated with accounting, tax or legal advisers where needed.

提交申報前，我們會按擬進行交易及所需批准核對公司記錄。重大變更會在需要時與會計、稅務或法律顧問協調。
