Frequently asked questions

常見問題

What should I do after receiving an estimated assessment?收到估計評稅後應怎樣處理？

The assessment and deadline should be reviewed immediately. Depending on the facts, an objection, holdover application or prompt filing of the outstanding return may be required.

應立即審閱評稅內容及期限。視乎情況，可能需要提出反對、申請暫緩繳稅，或盡快提交尚未呈交的報稅表。

When can provisional tax be held over?甚麼情況可以申請暫緩繳交暫繳稅？

Holdover may be available in specified circumstances, including a material reduction in assessable profits or the availability of tax losses. The application must be supported and filed on time.

在應評稅利潤大幅下降或有可用稅務虧損等指定情況下，可能可以申請暫緩繳稅。申請須有理據及在期限內提交。

What documents are normally required for an IRD enquiry?稅務局查詢一般需要哪些文件？

The documents depend on the questions raised, but commonly include accounting records, invoices, agreements, bank records, tax computations and written explanations of the commercial background.

所需文件視查詢內容而定，常見包括會計記錄、發票、協議、銀行記錄、稅項計算及商業背景的書面解釋。

Does the audit need to be completed before profits tax filing?利得稅申報前是否需要完成審計？

For most Hong Kong companies, the profits tax filing is prepared by reference to the audited financial statements. The audit and tax timetable should therefore be coordinated.

對大部分香港公司而言，利得稅申報以經審計財務報表為基礎，因此審計及稅務時間表應作協調。

稅務立場會按會計記錄、商業事實及支援文件作出考慮，並在提交回覆前清楚列明期限及所需行動。
