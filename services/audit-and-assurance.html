Frequently asked questions

常見問題

When should the audit process begin?審計程序應何時開始？

The audit should normally be planned before the reporting deadline becomes urgent. Early planning allows time to close the accounts, prepare supporting schedules and resolve accounting issues.

一般應在報告期限變得緊迫前開始規劃，讓管理層有足夠時間完成賬目、準備支援附表及處理會計問題。

What records are normally required?一般需要哪些會計記錄？

Common requirements include the general ledger, trial balance, bank statements, invoices, contracts, statutory records and supporting schedules for material balances.

常見資料包括總賬、試算表、銀行結單、發票、合約、法定記錄，以及重大結餘的支援附表。

Can you take over from an existing auditor?可以接替現任核數師嗎？

Yes. Subject to professional clearance and acceptance procedures, we can coordinate the change of auditor and agree a practical transition timetable.

可以。在完成專業查詢及接受委聘程序後，我們可協調更換核數師，並議定實際的交接時間表。

How are audit and profits tax filing coordinated?審計與利得稅申報如何協調？

The audited financial statements and audit adjustments form the basis of the profits tax computation. We coordinate the two workstreams so that the accounting and tax positions remain consistent.

經審計財務報表及審計調整是利得稅計算的基礎。我們會協調兩項工作，確保會計及稅務立場一致。

Our approach

服務方針

The audit timetable is coordinated with profits tax filing, board approvals and group reporting where applicable. Significant accounting or reporting issues are raised early rather than left to finalisation.

審計時間表會在適用情況下與利得稅申報、董事會批准及集團報告協調。重大會計或報告問題會及早提出，而非留待最後階段處理。
