Our work

工作範圍

We coordinate year end accounts, audit schedules and reporting matters relevant to licensed corporations, while working with management and the compliance function on the required information flow.

我們協調持牌法團的年結賬目、審計附表及相關報告事項，並與管理層及合規職能合作處理所需資料流程。

Frequently asked questions

常見問題

When should the annual audit process begin?周年審計程序應何時開始？

Planning should begin early enough to align year end closing, management accounts, financial resources considerations, audit work and regulatory submission deadlines.

應及早規劃，以協調年結、管理賬目、財政資源考量、審計工作及監管申報期限。

What records are normally required?一般需要哪些記錄？

Common requirements include the general ledger, trial balance, management accounts, bank and custodian records, fee calculations, expense schedules, regulatory returns and supporting documents.

常見資料包括總賬、試算表、管理賬目、銀行及託管記錄、費用計算、開支附表、監管申報表及支援文件。

What does FRR related accounting support cover?財政資源規則相關會計支援包括甚麼？

It covers the accounting information and balance sheet items relevant to financial resources reporting. Regulatory interpretation remains the responsibility of management and the compliance function, with specialist advice obtained where needed.

支援範圍包括與財政資源申報相關的會計資料及資產負債表項目。監管詮釋仍由管理層及合規職能負責，並在需要時取得專業意見。

Can you assist with the SFC audit questionnaire?可以協助證監會審計問卷嗎？

We can support the accounting and audit coordination aspects, including the relevant financial information and supporting documentation. Management remains responsible for the completeness and accuracy of the submission.

我們可支援會計及審計協調部分，包括相關財務資料及支援文件。管理層仍須對申報內容的完整性及準確性負責。

Our approach

服務方針

Finance, compliance and audit timetables are aligned early. Accounting information relevant to financial resources reporting is reviewed with clear responsibility retained by management for regulatory submissions.

財務、合規及審計時間表會及早協調。與財政資源申報相關的會計資料會作出審閱，而監管申報的責任仍由管理層承擔。
