Venture Partners CPA Limited 為四位合夥人主理的香港會計師事務所，服務私人企業、由企業擁有人直接管理的集團、海外股東持有的香港公司，以及證監會持牌法團。我們自2004年起，一直穩健發展。

Our Approach服務方針

Clear responsibility. Practical execution.

責任清晰，務實執行。

Our work is organised around four principles that apply across audit, tax, accounting and corporate compliance engagements.

我們以四項原則處理審計、稅務、會計及企業合規委聘。

01

Partner led

合夥人主理

A responsible partner leads each engagement and remains involved in the key decisions.

每項委聘均由指定合夥人主理，並參與重要判斷及決策。

02

Properly documented

妥善記錄

Key work, conclusions and follow up matters are documented clearly and consistently.

重要工作、結論及跟進事項均以清晰一致的方式記錄。

03

Coordinated work

工作協調

Audit, tax, accounting and corporate matters are coordinated rather than handled in isolation.

審計、稅務、會計及公司事務互相協調，而非各自分開處理。

04

Practical communication

務實溝通

Clients receive direct explanations, realistic timetables and clear next steps.

我們提供直接解釋、實際可行的時間表及清晰的下一步安排。

How We Work工作方式

Clear, organised and commercially relevant.

清晰、有條理、兼具商業視角。

Engagements run on documented timelines, written follow-up and proper supporting papers. We treat our deliverables as something a third party — a regulator, a banker, a future buyer — should be able to read and understand.

所有委聘均依書面時間表及跟進記錄推進，配以妥善支援文件。我們的交付成果，要能讓第三方（監管機構、銀行、未來投資者）翻閱即明白。

01

Scoping conversation

範圍商討

A partner discusses the engagement, the timeline, the supporting documents required and any matters likely to need attention.

合夥人與客戶商討委聘範圍、時間表、所需文件，以及可能需要特別關注的事項。

02

Planning & preparation

計劃及準備

A working timetable is set out in writing, with milestones, document lists and the people responsible on each side.

以書面訂立工作時間表，列明關鍵節點、文件清單，以及雙方各自負責的同事。

03

Fieldwork & review

實地工作及審閱

Audit, tax or accounting work is carried out by our team, with partner review and written communication of any issues identified.

由團隊執行審計、稅務或會計工作，並由合夥人審閱；任何發現事項均以書面與客戶溝通。

04

Sign-off & close-out

簽署及完成

For audit engagements, the responsible partner signs the auditor's report. Other engagements are closed with documented deliverables, outstanding matters and recommended follow-up actions.

就審計委聘而言，核數師報告由負責合夥人簽署。其他委聘則以書面記錄交付成果、未完成事項及建議跟進行動。
