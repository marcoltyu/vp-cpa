Audit審計

Preparing for a Hong Kong statutory audit

香港法定審計的準備工作

A smoother audit begins with a properly closed set of accounts and a realistic timetable. Before fieldwork starts, management should ensure that the trial balance agrees to the general ledger, bank balances are reconciled and material balance sheet items are supported by clear schedules.

順利的審計工作，始於已妥善完成年結的賬目及實際可行的時間表。實地工作開始前，管理層應確保試算表與總賬一致、銀行結餘已完成對賬，並為重大資產負債表項目準備清晰附表。

  • Agree the reporting and tax filing timetable early及早議定報告及稅務申報時間表
  • Prepare bank, receivable, payable, fixed asset and related party schedules準備銀行、應收、應付、固定資產及關連方附表
  • Collect significant contracts, board approvals and supporting documents整理重大合約、董事會批准及支援文件
  • Identify unusual transactions and accounting estimates before fieldwork在實地工作前識別不尋常交易及會計估算

Taxation稅務

What to do after receiving an IRD estimated assessment

收到稅務局估計評稅後應如何處理

An estimated assessment should not be ignored. The notice, filing status and statutory deadline should be reviewed immediately. Depending on the circumstances, the appropriate response may involve filing the outstanding return, lodging an objection, applying for holdover of tax or providing supporting information to the Inland Revenue Department.

估計評稅不應被忽視。收到通知後，應立即審閱評稅內容、報稅狀況及法定期限。視乎情況，合適的處理方式可能包括提交尚未呈交的報稅表、提出反對、申請暫緩繳稅，或向稅務局提供支援資料。

  • Check the objection and payment deadlines核對反對及繳稅期限
  • Complete outstanding accounts, audit and tax filings where required在需要時完成尚未完成的賬目、審計及稅務申報
  • Prepare a properly supported explanation of the tax position就稅務立場準備有充分支持的解釋
  • Consider whether a holdover application is available考慮是否符合申請暫緩繳稅的條件

Regulated Entities受規管實體

Year end accounting and audit checklist for SFC licensed corporations

證監會持牌法團年結會計及審計清單

For a licensed corporation, year end closing should be coordinated across finance, compliance and audit. Management accounts, fee income, accruals, tax provisions, receivables, payables and other balances relevant to financial resources reporting should be reviewed before the audit timetable becomes compressed.

持牌法團的年結工作，應由財務、合規及審計三方面協調。管理賬目、費用收入、應計、稅項撥備、應收、應付及其他與財政資源申報相關的結餘，應在審計時間表變得緊迫前完成審閱。

  • Reconcile management accounts to the general ledger將管理賬目與總賬完成對賬
  • Review fee calculations, accrued expenses and tax balances審閱費用計算、應計開支及稅項結餘
  • Prepare audit schedules and supporting documentation準備審計附表及支援文件
  • Coordinate outstanding matters with the compliance function與合規職能協調未完成事項
