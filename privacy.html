Privacy notice

私隱聲明

This notice explains how Venture Partners CPA Limited handles personal data received through this website and related enquiries.

本聲明說明 Venture Partners CPA Limited 如何處理透過本網站及相關查詢所收到的個人資料。

Information we may collect

我們可能收集的資料

When you contact us by email or telephone, we may receive your name, contact details, organisation, the nature of your enquiry and any information you choose to provide. Technical information may also be collected by the website hosting provider or analytics services if these are enabled.

當您透過電郵或電話聯絡本所時，我們可能收到您的姓名、聯絡資料、所屬機構、查詢性質，以及您選擇提供的其他資料。如網站啟用託管或分析服務，相關服務供應商亦可能收集技術資料。

How we use the information

資料用途

We use the information to respond to enquiries, consider potential engagements, provide professional services, maintain business records, comply with legal and professional obligations and protect our legitimate business interests.

我們會使用相關資料回覆查詢、考慮潛在委聘、提供專業服務、保存業務記錄、遵守法律及專業責任，以及保障本所的合法業務權益。

Disclosure and retention

披露及保留

Personal data may be shared with service providers, professional advisers, regulators or public authorities where reasonably required or legally permitted. We retain personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose and applicable obligations.

在合理需要或法律容許的情況下，個人資料可能會向服務供應商、專業顧問、監管機構或公共機構披露。我們只會在相關用途及適用責任所合理需要的期間內保留個人資料。

Cookies and analytics

Cookies 及網站分析

This static website does not require cookies for its core operation. If analytics or other tracking tools are introduced, this notice will be updated and additional consent measures will be considered where appropriate.

本靜態網站的核心運作毋須使用 Cookies。如日後加入網站分析或其他追蹤工具，本聲明將作更新，並會在適當情況下考慮額外同意措施。

Contact us

聯絡我們

For privacy related enquiries, please contact us at info@vp-cpa.com or (852) 2156 1234.

如有私隱相關查詢，請電郵至 info@vp-cpa.com 或致電 (852) 2156 1234。
