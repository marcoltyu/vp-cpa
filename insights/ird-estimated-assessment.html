Taxation稅務

What to do after receiving an IRD estimated assessment

收到稅務局估計評稅後應如何處理

An estimated assessment is normally issued because the Inland Revenue Department does not have a completed return or sufficient information to assess the actual position. It creates real objection and payment deadlines and should be reviewed immediately.

稅務局通常會在未收到已填妥報稅表或缺乏足夠資料評定實際稅務狀況時發出估計評稅。該評稅會產生實際反對及繳稅期限，應立即審閱。

Check the notice and deadline

核對通知及期限

Confirm the assessment year, assessed profits, tax payable, objection deadline and payment dates. Also check whether the underlying profits tax return, audited financial statements or tax computation remain outstanding.

確認課稅年度、評定利潤、應繳稅款、反對期限及繳稅日期，並核對相關利得稅報稅表、經審計財務報表或稅項計算是否尚未提交。

Decide the immediate filing action

決定即時申報行動

Where a return is outstanding, completing the accounts, audit and tax filing may be the most important step. An objection does not replace the need to submit the underlying information requested by the Department.

如報稅表尚未提交，完成賬目、審計及稅務申報可能是最重要的步驟。提出反對並不能取代提交稅務局所需基本資料的責任。

Consider objection and holdover separately

分開考慮反對及暫緩繳稅

An objection challenges the assessment. A holdover application deals with payment while the matter is being resolved. Each has its own requirements, supporting basis and deadline.

反對是對評稅提出爭議；暫緩繳稅申請則處理爭議期間的付款安排。兩者各有不同要求、支持理據及期限。

Prepare a supported explanation

準備有支持的解釋

The response should reconcile the accounting results to the tax position and explain material adjustments, losses, offshore claims or other matters with relevant documents.

回覆應將會計結果與稅務立場作出調節，並就重大調整、虧損、離岸申索或其他事項提供相關文件及解釋。

Information to have ready

建議預備的資料

  • Estimated assessment and demand note估計評稅及繳稅通知
  • Relevant profits tax return相關利得稅報稅表
  • Latest accounts or management accounts最新賬目或管理賬目
  • Audit status and expected completion date審計狀況及預計完成日期
  • Tax computation and loss schedules稅項計算及虧損附表
  • Previous assessments and correspondence過往評稅及往來文件
  • Supporting documents for material claims重大申索的支援文件
