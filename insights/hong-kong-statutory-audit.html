Audit審計

Preparing for a Hong Kong statutory audit

香港法定審計的準備工作

A smoother audit usually reflects preparation completed before fieldwork begins. The objective is not to produce more documents than necessary, but to make sure the accounting records are closed, reconciled and supported in a form that management and the audit team can follow.

審計工作是否順利，通常取決於實地工作開始前的準備。重點並非製作過量文件，而是確保會計記錄已完成年結、對賬及具備清晰支援，讓管理層及審計團隊均可追蹤。

Start with a realistic timetable

先訂立實際可行的時間表

The audit, financial statement approval and profits tax filing dates should be considered together. A timetable should allow time for account closing, information preparation, audit queries, management review and final approval.

審計、財務報表批准及利得稅申報日期應一併考慮。時間表應預留完成賬目、準備資料、回覆審計查詢、管理層審閱及最終批准所需時間。

Close and reconcile the accounting records

完成年結及會計記錄對賬

The trial balance should agree to the general ledger. Bank accounts, receivables, payables, fixed assets, loans, related party balances and tax balances should be reconciled before the audit team relies on them.

試算表應與總賬一致。銀行、應收、應付、固定資產、貸款、關連方及稅項結餘，應在審計團隊使用前完成對賬。

Prepare focused supporting schedules

準備聚焦的支援附表

Schedules should explain the composition and movement of material balances and link back to the ledger. Significant contracts, board approvals, invoices and external confirmations should be organised around those schedules.

附表應說明重大結餘的組成及變動，並可追溯至總賬。重大合約、董事會批准、發票及外部確認亦應按相關附表整理。

Identify judgemental matters early

及早識別需要判斷的事項

Impairment, provisions, revenue recognition, related party arrangements, foreign currency balances and unusual transactions often require discussion before finalisation. Early attention reduces last minute adjustments.

減值、撥備、收入確認、關連方安排、外幣結餘及不尋常交易，通常需要在完成報告前討論。及早處理可減少最後階段的調整。

Information to have ready

建議預備的資料

  • Final trial balance and general ledger最終試算表及總賬
  • Bank reconciliations and statements銀行對賬及結單
  • Receivable and payable ageing應收及應付賬齡
  • Fixed asset and loan schedules固定資產及貸款附表
  • Related party balances and transactions關連方結餘及交易
  • Major contracts and board approvals重大合約及董事會批准
  • Tax computation information and prior assessments稅項計算資料及過往評稅
