Year end checklist for SFC licensed corporations

證監會持牌法團年結工作清單

A licensed corporation year end brings together accounting close, audit preparation, financial resources information and regulatory reporting. The work is easier to control when finance, compliance and the audit team agree the information flow and timetable before the reporting period closes.

持牌法團的年結工作同時涉及會計結賬、審計準備、財政資源資料及監管報告。財務、合規及審計團隊如在報告期結束前議定資料流程及時間表，整體工作會更易管理。

Align the reporting timetable

協調報告時間表

Map the year end close, management accounts, audit fieldwork, financial statement approval and regulatory submission dates. Responsibility for each information set should be clear.

列出年結、管理賬目、審計工作、財務報表批准及監管申報日期，並清楚界定各項資料的負責人。

Close the accounting records

完成會計年結

Reconcile bank and custodian records, fee income, accrued expenses, receivables, payables, related party balances, tax provisions and other material accounts.

對銀行及託管記錄、費用收入、應計開支、應收、應付、關連方結餘、稅項撥備及其他重大賬項進行對賬。

Review financial resources information

審閱財政資源資料

Accounting balances relevant to financial resources reporting should be identified and supported. Management and compliance remain responsible for regulatory interpretation and submission completeness.

應識別及支持與財政資源申報相關的會計結餘。監管詮釋及申報完整性的責任仍由管理層及合規職能承擔。

Prepare the audit file efficiently

有效準備審計資料

Audit schedules should link to the ledger and explain material movements. Management should identify regulatory correspondence, board approvals and unusual transactions that may affect the audit.

審計附表應可追溯至總賬並解釋重大變動。管理層亦應識別可能影響審計的監管往來、董事會批准及不尋常交易。

Information to have ready

建議預備的資料

  • Final management accounts and general ledger最終管理賬目及總賬
  • Bank and custodian reconciliations銀行及託管對賬
  • Fee income calculations and agreements費用收入計算及協議
  • Accrued expenses and payable schedules應計開支及應付附表
  • Tax provision and tax filing status稅項撥備及申報狀況
  • Financial resources related balances財政資源相關結餘
  • Regulatory returns and correspondence監管申報表及往來文件
  • Board approvals and compliance confirmations董事會批准及合規確認
